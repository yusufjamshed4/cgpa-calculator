Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Zero Server-Side Data Collection

At CGPA Studio, your academic privacy is our highest priority. All calculations—including subject names, credit hours, grade points, and percentage conversions—are performed entirely inside your browser (Client-Side) using JavaScript. We do not transmit, store, or save your academic details to any external server or database.

2. Cross-Platform Storage & Local Execution

Whether you access this web app on Android, iOS, or Windows devices, the system processes your inputs locally. No persistent tracking cookies are used to track your personal identity or activity across other websites.

3. Third-Party Services & CDNs

This website utilizes external Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), such as Tailwind CSS and Google Fonts, to deliver UI assets smoothly. These standard services may log basic HTTP headers (such as IP address and user-agent string) purely for network security and content delivery purposes.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding the design, security, or data handling practices of CGPA Studio, feel free to reach out to the project maintainer.
